QUICK PREVIEW (no tools needed)
1. Extract this folder, then open index.html in Chrome.
2. To change text, open content.js in VS Code, edit, save, refresh the page.
3. Put your photo in the images folder and write 'images/portrait.png' in content.js (site.portrait).

This is a ready-made copy for looking at and editing content. It is NOT the project you deploy.
To put the site online use ethel-devs-react.zip and follow "Chromebook / no terminal" in its README.md.
Fonts need an internet connection.
